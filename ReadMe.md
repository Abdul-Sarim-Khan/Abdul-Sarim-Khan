### About Me & Experience

I am passionate about securing infrastructures and pushing the boundaries of AI in cybersecurity. When I am not mastering cloud security, I am engineering solutions for anomaly detection and network routing.

- **Currently Building:** **FLARE** (Federated Learning for Anomaly Recognition and Explainability)—a distributed XDR system using FastAPI and custom endpoint agents.
- **Research & Academia:** First-authored **Adaptive Priority Partitioning (APP)**, a routing framework for green last-mile delivery networks. Previously a Research Assistant at **ORIC** focusing on Algorithm Design.
- **Industry Experience:** Former Information Security Intern at **Kunjisoft Technologies**, specializing in IoT Security.
- **Community & Leadership:** Senior Administrator & Challenge Creator for the **Cyber Trihack Challenge** (Cryptography & Digital Forensics). 
- **Currently Learning:** Microsoft & AWS Cloud Security, Penetration Testing, and AI-Driven Threat Detection.
- **Fun Fact:** I enjoy breaking things (ethically) just to secure them better!
